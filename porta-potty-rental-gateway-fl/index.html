Frequently Asked Questions

1. How far in advance should I book a portable toilet rental?

For standard events or construction needs, we recommend booking at least 5–7 days in advance. For large events, weddings, or multi-unit orders, 2–3 weeks is ideal. That said, we do accommodate last-minute requests whenever our inventory and routing allow — call us and we'll do our best.

2. What's included in a standard porta potty rental?

Every standard unit comes stocked with toilet paper, hand sanitizer, and a deodorizing solution. Units are delivered pre-cleaned and sanitized. For longer rental periods, scheduled servicing is included according to your agreed plan.

3. Do you offer handicap-accessible portable restrooms?

Yes. We carry ADA-compliant portable restrooms that feature wider entry doors, interior handrails, and ground-level placement. These are available nationwide and can be added to any order.

4. Can I rent a portable toilet for just one day?

Absolutely. We offer one-day porta potty rental options for parties, small gatherings, and short-term events. Pricing is clear upfront with no hidden fees.

5. What's the difference between a standard porta potty and a luxury portable restroom?

Standard units are functional and clean — appropriate for most outdoor events and job sites. Luxury units include flushing toilets, running water, climate control, lighting, mirrors, and upscale interior finishes. They're ideal for weddings, VIP events, and any occasion where guest comfort is a priority.
